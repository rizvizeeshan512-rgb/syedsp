One country.
Many worlds.

From old harbour towns to high mountain terraces and the open desert, let the landscape set your pace.

Where to begin

Four places to feel.

Build in time between stops. Oman’s changing scenery is part of the experience.

01

Capital · Coast

Muscat

Explore the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, the Mutrah Corniche and souq, and the city’s mix of sea views, museums, and historic quarters.

Architecture · Markets · Sea
02

The Hajar Mountains

Jebel Akhdar

Highland villages, terraced farms, and sweeping views make this a striking change of pace. Check access and road requirements before setting out.

Hiking · Villages · Highlands
03

Eastern sands

Wahiba Sands

Open skies and shifting dunes create a memorable desert landscape. Arrange a knowledgeable local guide and suitable transport for remote routes.

Desert · Stargazing · Heritage
04

Southern Oman

Dhofar

Frankincense heritage, coastal cliffs, and the seasonal green landscapes of khareef offer a different face of Oman.

Coast · Frankincense · Khareef

Make the most of the road

Travel well.

Keep a flexible plan and follow local advice as conditions change.

01 / WHEN

Choose your season

October through April is generally milder for travel. Dhofar’s khareef, typically June to September, brings seasonal rain and greener scenery.

02 / HOW

Plan your distances

Journeys between regions can take time. Check current road conditions, carry water, and arrange appropriate transport for remote areas.

03 / WHAT

Pack respectfully

Light, modest clothing works well. At religious sites, follow posted visitor rules and local guidance.
