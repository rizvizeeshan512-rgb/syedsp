Chapter one · The story

History written
across the land.

A crossroads of sea routes and desert paths, Oman has connected people and ideas for thousands of years.

A long view

Rooted here.
Connected everywhere.

Oman’s geography shaped its story. Mountains protected settlements, oases made agriculture possible, and a long coastline opened the way to the wider Indian Ocean.

Archaeological sites across the country reveal communities that traded copper, frankincense, and other goods with distant neighbours. Omani sailors later built strong maritime connections with East Africa, India, and the Gulf. In the interior, falaj channels carried water to farms and towns through a careful system of shared stewardship.

Forts, watchtowers, and old trading quarters are reminders of local skill and enterprise. Today, Oman continues to preserve these landmarks while building a modern society shaped by its own heritage.

A landscape of memory

The past is not far away here. It is part of the road, the water, and the welcome.
